# Changelog

## 2.0.0 - 2026-09-12

- Duels: challenge a friend to the same traps on the same seed, from
  the new duel dock under the game, `::duel <name> trappy`, or a
  right-click on a chatbox name. Best round or average, 1, 3 or 5 games,
  any difficulty.
- Play when you like: accepting creates the duel, and each of you plays
  your games from the dock inside a five-minute idle window. The relay
  replays every run itself to verify the score; ties go to sudden death.
- Compete: one game against a random opponent at your difficulty, with
  an Elo rating and a verified top-scores board per difficulty.
- Your record: duels won and lost, head-to-head per friend, compete
  record and ratings, last 20 duels.
- All of it opt-in through the AKAddons relay (your display name, a
  hash of your account made on your client so the name stays yours, your
  duel results, and the address you connect from are all it sees). The relay replays every run to verify the
  score, reveals seeds one game at a time, and limits rating farming.
  Settings to hide your name from the boards and to block challengers.
- Duel games simulate on a fixed 242x480 world (letterboxed to your
  panel) so both players face identical layouts whatever their panel
  size.
